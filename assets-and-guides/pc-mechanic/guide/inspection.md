# Vehicle Inspection & Diagnostics

## Understanding the Inspection System

**How inspection works:**

- Connect to vehicle and navigate to "Servicing" app in tablet
- Initially, all servicing parts show as **"Unknown"** (not yet inspected)
- Use diagnostic tools to inspect parts and reveal their condition
- Once inspected, parts become **"Known"** to the mechanic
- After time/mileage threshold, values become **"Last Known"** and require re-inspection

**Re-inspection triggers:**

- **Time Threshold:** 1 day (24 hours) since last inspection
- **Mileage Threshold:** 20% mileage increase from last inspection

## OBD Scanner

**Tool Required:** OBD-II Scanner

**Inspects:**

- Engine Oil
- Clutch
- EV Motor (for electric vehicles)

1. Ensure you have OBD Scanner item in inventory
2. Connect to vehicle on tablet
3. Navigate to Servicing app
4. Click "OBD Diagnostics" button
5. Complete the minigame successfully
6. Parts become known and display current values

![OBD Scanner Tool](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Inspection/3.png)
