# Getting Started

## Using the Testing Resource

The PC Mechanic Tester provides quick access to jobs, vehicles, and items needed for testing. Follow these steps to begin:

**Step 1: Get a Job**

1. Locate the testing NPC at LS Customs (coords: `-356.49, -131.88, 39.43`)
2. Walk into the yellow marker and press `[E]`
3. Select "Hire Yourself" and choose Manager or Mechanic role
4. You'll be added as an employee to the mechanic shop
5. Check your role and permissions have been applied

![NPC Hiring Menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/GettingStarted/1.png)

## Spawn a Vehicle

**Two vehicle types available:**

1. Interact with the tester NPC again
2. **Test Vehicle:** Unowned vehicle for quick testing (auto-deleted when idle)
3. **Owned Vehicle:** Saved to your garage, modifications persist
4. Select your preferred vehicle type
5. Vehicle will spawn nearby

![Vehicle Spawn Options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/GettingStarted/2.png)

## Get Items

**Available item categories:**

- **Inspection Tools:** Tablet, OBD scanner, gauges, multimeter, etc.
- **Servicing Items:** Oil, filters, brake pads, tyres, etc.
- **Tuning Parts:** Engines, turbos, drivetrains, etc.
- **Order Items:** Cosmetic parts, wheels, body kits, etc.

> **Quick Tip:** Use "Get All" options to receive complete item sets at once.
