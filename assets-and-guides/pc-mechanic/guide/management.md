# Management

## Accessing Management Panel

1. Open tablet, click "Management" section
2. Verify proper permissions (owner/manager role)
3. Management panel shows business overview
4. Review available management options

> **Warning:** Most features require elevated permissions.

## Viewing Shop Balance

1. In management, locate "Balance" or "Funds" section
2. View current shop balance
3. Check transaction history if available
4. Note currency type (cash, bank, society)

## Depositing/Withdrawing Funds

**Deposit:**

1. Click "Deposit" or "Add Funds"
2. Choose source: Personal Cash or Bank
3. Enter amount to deposit
4. Confirm transaction
5. Shop balance increases, personal decreases

**Withdraw:**

1. Click "Withdraw" button
2. Enter amount to withdraw
3. Confirm withdrawal
4. Funds transferred to personal account
5. Shop balance decreases

## Managing Employees

1. Navigate to "Employees" or "Staff" section
2. View list of employees with roles
3. See stats, hire date, performance
4. Use sorting and filtering options
5. Access options: hire, fire, promote

## Configuring Shop Settings

1. Open "Settings" in management panel
2. Adjust shop-specific config options
3. Set pricing multipliers or commission rates
4. Configure employee permissions if available
5. Save changes

## Viewing Shop Statistics

1. Check "Stats" or "Dashboard" section
2. Review total orders completed
3. See revenue over time periods
4. Check customer satisfaction ratings
5. Analyze employee performance metrics
