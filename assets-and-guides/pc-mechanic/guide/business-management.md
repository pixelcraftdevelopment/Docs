# Business Management

## Shop Balance Overview

1. Open tablet, navigate to Management section
2. View current shop balance (cash/bank/society)
3. Check transaction history if available
4. Monitor income from completed orders
5. Track expenses from part purchases

![Balance Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/BusinessManagement/1.png)

## Deposit Funds

1. Click "Deposit" or "Add Funds" button
2. Choose source: Personal Cash or Bank account
3. Enter amount you want to deposit
4. Confirm the transaction
5. Shop balance increases, personal funds decrease

![Deposit Funds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/BusinessManagement/2.png)

## Withdraw Funds

1. Click "Withdraw" button in management
2. Enter withdrawal amount
3. Confirm withdrawal action
4. Funds transferred to your personal account
5. Shop balance decreases by withdrawal amount

> **Note:** Withdrawal permissions may be restricted to Owner/Manager roles.

![Withdraw Funds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/BusinessManagement/3.png)

## Employee Management

1. Navigate to "Employees" section in management
2. View list of all shop employees with roles
3. Check employee stats and performance data
4. Hire new employees (requires Owner/Manager)
5. Fire employees or change roles (requires Owner/Manager)
6. Track employee hours and completed orders

![Employee Management](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/BusinessManagement/4.png)

## Shop Statistics

1. Open Stats or Dashboard in management panel
2. Review total orders completed this week/month
3. Check revenue and profit margins
4. View customer satisfaction ratings from feedback
5. Analyze employee performance metrics

![Shop Statistics](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/BusinessManagement/5.png)
