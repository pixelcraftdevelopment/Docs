# Vehicle Connection

## Connecting to a Vehicle

1. Position yourself near a vehicle (within 4m default)
2. Open the tablet if not already open
3. Look for "Nearby Vehicles" section
4. Click on the vehicle you want to connect to
5. Vehicle freezes in place and connection info appears
6. View vehicle type, mileage, and servicing status

> **Warning:** Vehicle must be stopped (speed < 1.0) to connect.

![Vehicle Connection Process](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/VehicleConnection/1.png)

## Vehicle Information Display

After connecting, you'll see:

- **Vehicle Type:** Car, Bike, or Other
- **Archetype Name:** Model identifier
- **Electric Status:** Gas or Electric vehicle
- **Tuning Config:** Available modifications
- **Mileage:** Distance traveled (km or miles)
- **Servicing State:** Component degradation levels
- **Nitrous Data:** Bottles installed/filled

## Disconnecting from Vehicle

1. Locate the disconnect button on the tablet
2. Click disconnect/close vehicle connection
3. Vehicle unfreezes and is released
4. Tablet returns to main dashboard
5. Verify vehicle is no longer frozen

> **Tip:** Always disconnect when done to allow vehicles to move freely.
