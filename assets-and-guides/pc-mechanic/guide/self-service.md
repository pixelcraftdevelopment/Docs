# Vehicle Customization Orders

## Opening the Modification Menu

1. Drive your vehicle to a mechanic shop
2. Press `[E]` to open the GTA modification menu
3. Browse all available customization categories
4. Navigate through: Performance, Cosmetics, Wheels, Respray, etc.

![Modification Menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/CustomizationOrders/1.png)

## Selecting Parts and Adding to Cart

1. Click on any part to preview it and add to cart
2. Review the part's price and appearance on your vehicle
3. Click on "Stock" to remove the part from cart
4. Continue browsing and adding more parts
5. Build your complete customization order

> **Note:** Parts are only previewed, not applied yet.

![Part Preview and Cart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/CustomizationOrders/2.png)

## Placing the Order

1. Review all parts in your cart
2. Check the total price
3. Choose payment method: Bank or Cash
4. Confirm the purchase to place the order
5. Order is now placed and ready for processing

> **Important:** Modifications are NOT applied yet — you've only placed the order. A mechanic must complete the work order to apply the modifications.

![Order Checkout](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/CustomizationOrders/3.png)

## Self-Service Shops (Alternative)

**Self-service shops work differently:**

- No work orders are placed
- Modifications are applied directly and immediately
- Payment is instant upon selection
- No mechanic needed to complete the work

> **Tip:** Use self-service shops for quick modifications without waiting for mechanic approval.
