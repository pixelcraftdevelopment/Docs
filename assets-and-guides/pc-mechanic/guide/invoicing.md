# Invoicing

## Creating an Invoice

1. Open tablet, navigate to "Invoices" or "Billing"
2. Click "Create New Invoice"
3. Add items: description, quantity, unit price
4. Total calculates automatically
5. Save invoice as draft

![Invoice Creation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Invoicing/1.png)

## Sending Invoice to Customer

1. Open or create invoice
2. Click "Send Invoice"
3. Select customer/player from nearby players list
4. Add optional description or note
5. Confirm to send invoice

> **Note:** Customer receives notification and must accept/pay.

![Send Invoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Invoicing/2.png)

## Managing Outstanding Invoices

1. View list of outstanding (unpaid) invoices
2. Sort by date, customer, or amount
3. Use pagination for large lists
4. Check status: Sent, Paid, Overdue
5. Take action: Resend, Delete, View Details

![Invoice List](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Invoicing/3.png)

## Paying Invoice (Customer Side)

1. Receive invoice notification
2. Invoice details appear in NUI
3. Review items and total amount
4. Choose payment: Cash, Bank, or Society
5. Confirm payment
6. Funds transferred to mechanic shop

![Payment Method](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Invoicing/4.png)

## Deleting Invoices

1. Open invoice to delete
2. Click "Delete" or trash icon
3. Confirm deletion
4. Invoice permanently removed

> **Warning:** Deleted invoices cannot be recovered.

![Delete Invoice](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/Invoicing/5.png)
