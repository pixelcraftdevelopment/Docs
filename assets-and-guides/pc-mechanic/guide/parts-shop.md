# Parts Shop (NUI Catalog)

## Opening Parts Catalog

1. Open tablet and navigate to "Shop" section
2. The NUI-based parts catalog opens
3. Browse categories: Performance, Cosmetics, Wheels, etc.
4. Use search bar to find specific parts
5. Filter by category or price range

![Parts Catalog](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/1.png)

## Adding Items to Cart

1. Click on a part to view details
2. Check part price and availability
3. Adjust quantity using +/- buttons
4. Click "Add to Cart" button
5. Cart icon shows number of items added

![Add to Cart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/2.png)

## Placing Bulk Orders

1. Add multiple items to cart
2. Click cart icon to review order
3. Verify quantities and total price
4. Choose payment method (shop funds or personal)
5. Confirm to place order
6. Items delivered to shop inventory/stash

![Bulk Order Cart](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/3.png)

![Confirm Bulk Order](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/4.png)

## Order History

1. Navigate to "Order History" tab
2. View all past parts purchases
3. Check order dates and amounts
4. Track spending on parts over time
5. Cancel pending orders if needed

![Order History](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/5.png)

![Order Details](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/PartsShop/6AAb.png)

## Stock Verification

1. Catalog shows current stock levels
2. System checks inventory space before purchase
3. Warns if insufficient storage capacity
4. Verifies cart items are still in stock
5. Updates stock in real-time during checkout
