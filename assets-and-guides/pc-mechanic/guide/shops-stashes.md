# Shops & Stashes

## Locating Shops and Stashes

1. Navigate to your mechanic shop location
2. Look for marked zones, peds, or markers
3. Shops marked with store icon
4. Stashes marked with box/storage icon
5. Approach zone/ped to see interaction prompt

## Accessing Parts Shop

1. Stand in shop zone or target shop ped
2. Press interaction key (E default) or use target menu
3. Shop menu opens showing available parts
4. Browse items with prices in configured currency
5. Note: employee status may be required

## Purchasing Parts

1. Click on item in shop menu
2. Enter quantity to purchase
3. Confirm purchase
4. System checks inventory space
5. Items added to inventory if successful

> **Note:** May deduct from shop balance or personal funds.

## Using Stash System

1. Approach stash zone or ped
2. Interact to open stash inventory
3. Transfer items between inventory and stash
4. Stashes shared among employees with permissions
5. Close stash when finished

> **Stash Naming:** Format: `mechanicId_stashName_index`

## NUI Parts Catalog (Advanced)

1. Open NUI-based parts catalog from tablet
2. Browse parts with filtering and search
3. Add multiple items to cart
4. Place bulk orders for delivery
5. Track order history and status
