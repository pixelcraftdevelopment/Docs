# Performance Tuning

## Installing Tuning Parts

**How to install performance tuning parts:**

1. Navigate to the Tuning app in your tablet
2. Connect to vehicle if not already connected
3. View various tuning categories and available parts
4. Select a part to view its specifications and effects on the vehicle
5. Press "Install" to begin the installation process
6. Complete the minigame to install the part
7. Performance changes apply immediately to the vehicle

## Available Tuning Parts

**Engines:**

- **I4 Turbo 2.5L:** Twin-turbo 2.5L engine reaching up to 100mph. Great for lightweight vehicles.
- **V6 3.3L:** Tuned V6 capable of 120mph. Balanced performance for mid-size sports cars.
- **V8 6.5L:** Naturally aspirated 6.5L V8 with awesome backfires and crackling exhaust sounds. High performance powerhouse.
- **V12 6.0L:** Massive 6L V12 reaching over 130mph. Only for vehicles that can realistically fit a V12.
- **Engine Replacement:** Complete engine rebuild for seized engines. Restores full functionality.

**Anti-Roll Bars:**

- **Sport Anti-Roll Bars:** Reduces body roll by 35% during cornering. Better control through turns.
- **Race Anti-Roll Bars:** Heavy-duty bars reducing body roll by 60%. Maximum cornering performance for aggressive track driving.

**Tyres:**

- **Slicks:** Maximum grip racing tyres with no tread. Optimal for dry track performance.
- **Semi-slicks:** Street-legal performance tyres with minimal tread. Enhanced grip in dry conditions.
- **Offroad:** All-terrain tyres for maximum traction on unpaved surfaces and rough terrain.

**Brakes:**

- **Ceramic Brakes:** Powerful carbon-ceramic brakes with immense stopping power. Shorter braking distances.

**Launch Control:**

- **Launch Control System:** Prevents wheelspin during launches with 70% improved traction. Perfect for drag racing.

**Drivetrains:**

- **AWD:** All-wheel drive with 50/50 power split. Balanced traction in all conditions.
- **RWD:** Rear-wheel drive for classic sports car handling and drift capability.
- **FWD:** Front-wheel drive optimized for traction and fuel efficiency.

**Drift Tuning:**

- **Drift Tuning Kit:** Complete drift setup with 58-degree steering angle, adjusted suspension, and power-oversteer characteristics.
