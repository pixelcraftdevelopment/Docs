# Tablet Basics

## Navigating the Interface

**All Available Apps:**

- **Orders:** View and manage customer work orders
- **Shop:** Access parts catalog and purchase items (NUI-based)
- **Management:** Business settings, employees, and funds management
- **Invoices:** Create and send customer bills
- **Diagnostics:** Vehicle inspection and servicing data display
- **TunePro:** Vehicle performance tuning and modifications app

![Navigation Menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/TabletBasics/1.png)

## Understanding the Tablet

1. Check the top bar for current time, weather, and location
2. Review shop balance and total orders count
3. Note your employee role and on-duty status
4. Familiarize yourself with navigation menu sections
5. Check for any notifications or alerts

![Dashboard Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/MechanicGuide/images/TabletBasics/2.png)

## Toggling Duty Status

1. Open the tablet and navigate to settings/profile
2. Find the "On Duty" or "Toggle Duty" switch
3. Click to toggle between on-duty and off-duty
4. Confirm the status change notification appears
5. Verify your duty status is reflected in the UI

> **Note:** Being on-duty may be required to access certain features.
