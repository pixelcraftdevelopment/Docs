# Studio & Earnings

The Studio tab is the creator dashboard. It only shows once you've enabled creator mode in Settings.

## What Studio Shows

* **Total earnings** — lifetime earnings, after the platform fee.
* **Available balance** — earnings sitting in your Fanly wallet, ready to withdraw.
* **Earnings history** — chronological log of every credit (sub, tip, PPV, paid DM), with the source fan and amount.
* **Pricing controls** — base monthly price, bundle discounts, trial length, promo settings.
* **DM permission** — set who can message you (open, subscribers, paid, closed).
* **Posts** — list of all your posts, with engagement counts and unlock counts.
* **Fan count** — current active subscribers + lifetime fan total.

## How You Earn

Every charge a fan makes credits your Fanly wallet:

* Subscription (monthly, bundle, or paid trial)
* Tips
* PPV unlocks
* Paid DM messages

The server takes a configurable platform percentage from each credit (default 10%) before it lands in your wallet. The remainder is yours.

## Withdrawing to Bank

Tap **Withdraw** to move your available balance to your in-game bank. The transfer is instant and you get a confirmation push.

The destination bank is whichever banking resource Fanly is integrated with — `pc-banking`, `qb-banking`, `okokBanking`, `Renewed-Banking`, `wasabi`, `jaksam`, `fd`, `tgg`, `crm`, `esx_addonaccount`, or your framework's native money handling. The server admin sets this.

## Pricing Tips

* Start at the lower end of the allowed range — fans are more willing to subscribe at $10 than $50.
* Run a trial when you're starting out — fans are more likely to convert to paid after they've seen your content.
* Use bundles to lock in long-term fans at a discount — better than losing them to month-by-month churn.
* Promos are most effective when time-limited — "50% off first month, this weekend only".

## Refunds

Refunds aren't automatic — once a charge has gone through (sub, PPV, tip, DM), the fan owns the access permanently and the earnings are yours. If you delete a PPV post after fans have unlocked it, no refund is issued.
