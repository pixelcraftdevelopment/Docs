# Composing Posts

{% hint style="info" %}
You need creator mode turned on to post. Enable it from **Settings → Become a Creator** and pick your @handle, display name, and pricing.
{% endhint %}

## Opening Compose

Tap the centre **Post** button in the bottom nav (FAB). The Compose screen opens.

## Fields

* **Body** — your text caption, up to the configured max length.
* **Media URL** — paste a direct link to an image or video. Supported extensions are configurable per server; default is `jpg / jpeg / png / gif / webp / avif` for images and `mp4 / webm` for video.
* **Visibility** — Free, Subscriber-only, or Pay-per-view.
* **Price** (PPV only) — per-post unlock price, bounded by the server's min/max PPV settings.

## Preview

Before publishing, the preview shows your post the way fans will see it (with the paywall lock applied if you set a price).

## Publishing

Tap **Publish**. The post appears at the top of your profile and lands in subscribers' Home feeds. Subscribers also get a phone push that you've posted (subject to their notification settings).

## Editing & Deleting

From your own profile or the Studio post list, open a post and use the menu to:

* **Delete** — removes the post permanently. Refunds for already-unlocked PPV are not automatic.
* **Pin** — keep a post at the top of your profile (one pinned slot).

{% hint style="warning" %}
There is no image upload in v1 — Compose accepts URLs only. For self-hosted media, point to your own CDN or asset host.
{% endhint %}
