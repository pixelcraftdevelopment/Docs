# Creator Profiles

## What's on a Profile

Every creator has a public profile page with:

* **Banner** + **avatar** + **display name** + **@handle**.
* **Bio** — short paragraph the creator writes.
* **Counts** — posts, fans, likes received.
* **Subscribe** button (or **Manage** if you're already subscribed).
* **Tip** button — send any amount within the configured tip range.
* **Message** button — opens a DM thread (if the creator allows DMs from your tier).
* **Posts grid** — chronological list of their posts, with locked posts shown as blurred previews.

## Subscribing

Tap **Subscribe** to open the bottom-sheet pricing tile. The sheet shows:

* The creator's monthly price.
* Bundle options (3 / 6 / 12 month) with the discount applied.
* A free trial offer, if available and you haven't used it before with this creator.
* An active promo (% off first month), if the creator has one running.

Pick a tier, confirm, and the charge runs against your bank. Your subscription kicks in immediately and the creator's full feed unlocks.

See [Subscriptions, Bundles & Trials](subscriptions.md) for the full breakdown of how each tier renews.

## Tipping

Tap **Tip**, pick a quick amount or enter a custom value, and confirm. Tips are one-shot — they go straight to the creator's earnings (minus the platform fee). The creator gets a phone push and a notification.

Tip range is bounded by the server's configured min/max — by default $1 to $5,000.

## Messaging

Tap **Message** to open or start a DM thread. Whether you can message depends on the creator's DM setting:

* **Open** — anyone can message.
* **Subscribers only** — you must hold an active subscription.
* **Paid** — every message you send costs a configurable fee.
* **Closed** — DMs disabled entirely.

## Following vs Subscribing

Following is free — you'll see the creator's free posts in your Home feed and get notified when they post. Subscribing costs money but unlocks all subscriber-only content, paid posts (still gated by per-post PPV unlocks), and full DM access where applicable.
