# Getting Started

## Opening the App

Fanly lives inside your phone. Open the phone, find the **Fanly** icon in the app launcher, and tap to open. The app works on every supported phone — lb-phone, yseries, gksphone, qs-smartphone (and pro), qb-phone, 17mov_Phone, and npwd.

The first screen is the Welcome page. From there you can either sign up for a brand-new account or sign in to an existing one.

## What Fanly Is

Fanly is a creator-subscription platform — think of it as the in-game version of a fan-supported social app:

* **Fans** browse creators, subscribe monthly, unlock paid posts, tip, and DM.
* **Creators** post free or paid content, set a monthly price, offer bundle discounts and trials, and earn money that withdraws straight to their in-game bank.

Every player starts as a fan. Creator mode is a setting you turn on when you're ready to start earning.

## The Five Tabs

The bottom navigation has five spots:

1. **Home** — your personalised ranked feed.
2. **Discover** — find new creators.
3. **Post** (centre, FAB) — quick shortcut to compose a new post (creator mode only).
4. **Messages** — your DM inbox.
5. **Profile** — your own profile, settings, and Studio dashboard.

## Top Bar

Every screen has a top bar with the **Fanly** brand on the left, a **bell** icon (notifications) and a **cog** icon (settings) on the right.

## Live Updates

When something changes in the background — a new subscriber, a tip arrives, a renewal goes through — the screen refreshes itself without you reopening anything. You also get a phone push (SMS / mail / native push depending on your phone resource).
