# Signing In

## First Time — Sign Up

On the Welcome screen, tap **Create Account**. You'll be asked for:

* **Username** — 3 to 24 characters, lowercase letters / numbers / underscores only. This is how you sign in. It's separate from your public **handle** (the @name other players see).
* **Password** — 4 to 64 characters.

Once submitted, you're signed in and dropped onto your Home feed. A 24-hour session token is saved on the phone so you don't have to log in again every time you open the app.

{% hint style="info" %}
Sign-up is tied to your character — only the character you're playing right now can register an account on Fanly. You can't sign up "as" someone else.
{% endhint %}

## Returning — Sign In

Tap **Sign In**, enter username + password. If you check **Remember me**, the phone keeps a 24-hour session and auto-resumes next time.

## Cross-Account Sign-In

If you sign in with **someone else's** username + password (and they shared them with you), Fanly adopts their persona for the rest of your session — your posts, DMs, tips, and earnings all attribute to that account. This makes it easy for shared cars, household co-owners, or trusted friends to manage one creator together.

A few safety rails apply:

* Sessions opened with someone else's creds stay in memory only — no token is saved on the phone, so closing it ends the borrowed session.
* If the borrowed account spends or earns money, charges and payouts route to the **real account owner's** bank, never to the player who's typing.
* Sign-up is not borrow-able — you can only register an account for your own character.

## Forgot Password

Tap **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen and enter the username. A reset code is sent over SMS to the **account owner's** phone (not the device requesting the reset). Once they tell you the code, enter it and choose a new password.

If the account owner is offline at the time, the reset is blocked until they're back in the city.

## Signing Out

Two options live in **Settings**:

* **Log out** — clears the in-memory session but keeps the saved 24-hour token, so the next open auto-resumes. Use this when you just want to switch accounts on the same phone.
* **Sign out everywhere** — revokes the token entirely. Next open requires a full username + password.
