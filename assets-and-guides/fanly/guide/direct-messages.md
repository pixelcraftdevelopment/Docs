# Direct Messages

## Inbox

The **Messages** tab shows your DM threads, newest activity at the top. Tap a thread to open it.

## Starting a Thread

Open any creator's profile and tap **Message**. Whether you can DM that creator depends on their DM setting:

| Setting | Who Can Send |
|---------|--------------|
| **Open** | Anyone |
| **Subscribers** | Active subscribers only |
| **Paid** | Anyone, but each message costs a fee the creator sets |
| **Closed** | DMs disabled entirely |

Creators set their preference in **Studio → Settings**.

## Paid Messages

When a creator has paid DMs enabled, the compose box shows the per-message fee. Hit send and the charge runs against your bank, then the message delivers to the creator. The creator earns the fee minus the platform percentage.

Paid messages also work the other way around — creators can send paid teaser messages to fans where the fan must pay to view the content. Standard text messages back and forth from the creator are free.

## Tipping in DMs

Inside a thread you can tap the tip icon to send a tip directly. The creator gets the standard tip-received push.

## Live Threads

When a new message arrives, the thread refreshes itself in the background and you get a phone push (subject to your notification settings).

## Message Limits

* Body text is bounded by the server's configured max length.
* Media in DMs is whitelisted by extension the same way post media is.
