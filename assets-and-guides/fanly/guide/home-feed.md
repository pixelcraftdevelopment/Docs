# Home Feed, Discover & Subscribed

## Home

The Home tab shows a personalised, ranked feed mixed from creators you subscribe to plus suggested posts from creators you might like. The mix is shaped by:

* **Freshness** — newer posts surface higher.
* **Engagement** — likes, comments, bookmarks, and shares lift posts.
* **Affinity** — the more you interact with a creator, the more of their posts you see.
* **Popularity** — broadly liked creators surface to people who don't yet follow them.
* **Fatigue** — posts you've already seen are dampened, and the feed avoids showing too many in a row from the same creator.

Pull down to refresh. Scroll to load more — the feed expands further as you go.

## Discover

The Discover tab is for finding **new** creators outside your subscribed list. You'll see:

* **Trending** creators — popular this week.
* **Search** — type a handle, display name, or keyword.

Tap any creator to open their profile.

## Subscribed

A filter on Home that shows only posts from creators you currently subscribe to (active monthly, bundle, or trial). Useful when your Home feed is mixing in too many recommendations.

## Bookmarks

Bookmarked posts get their own list — accessible from your profile menu. Bookmarking is private (creators don't see who bookmarked).
