# Settings

The cog icon in the top bar opens Settings.

## Profile

* **Display name** — how your name shows up across the app.
* **@handle** — your public username (3-32 characters, lowercase / digits / underscore). Must be unique. Different from your sign-in username.
* **Bio** — short paragraph on your profile.
* **Avatar URL** — direct link to your profile picture.
* **Banner URL** — direct link to your profile banner.

## Become a Creator

Toggle creator mode on. Once enabled, the **Studio** tab unlocks and the centre **Post** FAB lets you compose.

When you turn on creator mode, you'll be prompted to set:

* Base monthly price (within the server's allowed range)
* Optional bundle discounts (3 / 6 / 12 month)
* Optional trial length
* DM permission (open / subscribers / paid / closed)

You can change any of these later from Studio.

## Account & Security

* **Change password** — requires your current password, then a new one (4-64 characters).
* **Log out** — ends the in-memory session, keeps the saved 24-hour token, next open auto-resumes.
* **Sign out everywhere** — revokes the saved token. Next open requires full username + password.

## Notifications

Per-event toggles control which pushes you receive:

* New subscriber
* Subscription renewed / expired / renewal failed
* Tip received
* PPV unlocked
* DM received
* New post (from creators you follow)
* New comment

You can also pause all push notifications without disabling them individually.

## App

* **Theme** — single dark theme in v1. (Pure black canvas, signature blue accent.)
* **Language** — picks the locale used inside the app (server admin sets the default).
* **About** — version number and credits.
