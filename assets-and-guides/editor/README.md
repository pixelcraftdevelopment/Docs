# PC-Crafting-V2

PC-Crafting-V2 introduces a realistic and engaging system for crafting and repairing weapons. It goes beyond simple crafting, incorporating:

* **Weapon Degradation:** Weapons wear down with use, requiring repair and maintenance.
* **Part-Based System:** Weapons are composed of individual parts, each with its own health and impact on weapon performance.
* **Crafting Experience:** Players earn experience by crafting and repairing, unlocking new possibilities as they progress.
