# Weapon Packs

## What are Weapon Packs?

PC Crafting v2 ships with multiple interchangeable **Weapon Packs**. Each pack defines a unique collection of craftable weapons, fully optimized and mapped for the **3D assembly viewer**. Every weapon in a pack has its 3D model, individual parts, and visual slots precisely configured — when you place a barrel, grip, trigger, or stock on the crafting table, it physically attaches to the weapon model in real time.

Three packs are available, each offering a different set of weapon models suited to different server styles. All weapons across every pack are fully compatible with the crafting table's 3D interactive experience.

![Weapon Packs Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/CraftingGuide/images/WeaponPacks/1.png)

## Vanilla Pack

Uses the standard GTA V weapon models every player already knows. All weapons are the base in-game models — no custom streaming, no additional assets required. Every weapon is fully mapped to the 3D crafting viewer so each part (barrel, grip, trigger, stock, magazine, and more) visually attaches to the weapon as you place it on the table.

Safest, lightest choice for servers that want to avoid streaming custom assets while still delivering the full 3D crafting experience. Includes standard SMGs, pistols, rifles, shotguns, snipers, and machine guns — all of the GTA V classics.

![Vanilla Pack Weapons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/CraftingGuide/images/WeaponPacks/2.png)

## Custom PC Pack

Features weapons built specifically for the PixelCraft crafting interface. Custom-modeled weapons with more detailed 3D geometry, realistic part placements, and richer visual feedback during assembly. Every weapon model in this pack was designed from the ground up to look and feel great in the 3D viewer — you can clearly see each component slot in its correct position on the weapon.

This pack requires custom model streaming to be active on your server. Offers an enhanced crafting immersion compared to vanilla weapons, with more distinct part shapes and a polished assembly experience.

![Custom PC Pack Weapons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/CraftingGuide/images/WeaponPacks/3.png)

## Custom V3 Pack

Third-generation custom weapon model set. Building on the Custom PC Pack, V3 introduces further refined weapon geometry, additional part categories, and more granular part breakdowns — giving you more assembly steps and a more detailed crafting experience in the 3D viewer.

V3 weapons are designed for servers that want the most immersive and detailed weapon assembly experience available. Each weapon model has been updated with improved visual fidelity and expanded part slots compared to earlier custom packs.

![Custom V3 Pack Weapons](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/CraftingGuide/images/WeaponPacks/4.png)
