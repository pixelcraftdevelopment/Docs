## **Description**

Dive into an exhilarating role-playing experience with our advanced GTA V script, designed for server owners seeking to enhance gameplay. Players can embody high-risk security contractors or cunning criminals, each with specialized tablets for strategic operations. Contractors coordinate VIP protection and manage teams, while criminals plot and execute high-stakes heists.

Our script emphasizes balanced, dynamic gameplay with a focus on team strategy and individual skill. It's optimized for smooth performance, ensuring a seamless and immersive experience. Elevate your server with a narrative that evolves with each decision, captivating your community with every mission and operation. Transform your server into a realm of strategy, teamwork, and thrilling action today!



## **Features**

* Tablet to start job
* Experience based job completion
* Configurable shop with purchasable items with full working cart
* Server sided stock of all items
* Fully multiplayer job
* Separate tablet for criminal side
* Multi faceted roleplay experience(Extended RP for different types of jobs eg. EMS, mechanic, police, criminals, civilians)
* Regular updates to be added
