# Getting Started with Banking

## Opening the Banking UI

Three ways to open the banking interface:

1. **Walk into a bank** - eight branches across the map (Fleeca, Maze, Pacific Standard). Use the target prompt on the teller to open online banking with full services.
1. **Type /bank** - opens online banking. If you stand within ~50m of a branch, it opens that branch's services. Otherwise it opens a default branch.
1. **Use a card item** - flashes a card overlay on screen (does not open the UI). Position is configurable.

![Banking UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/GettingStarted/1.png)

## Three Banks, Three Tiers

- **Fleeca** - neighborhood budget bank. Free everyday checking, low limits, lots of branches.
- **Maze Bank** - corporate mid-tier. Better limits, small monthly fee, business-friendly.
- **Pacific Standard** - flagship prestige bank. Highest limits and best rates, premium fees.

Each bank offers its own checking, savings, cards, and loan products. Per-bank fees and limits are tunable in the account configs.

## Your First Time

1. Personal checking accounts at every bank are created automatically when you first register, so you start with a balance of zero across all three.
1. If banking password is enabled, register a password on first login. Sessions remember you for 24 hours by default.
1. Deposit cash to start using transfers, savings, cards, and loans.
