# Loans & Credit Score

## Loan Types

Four products, each bank offers a different mix:

- **Personal** - small fast loans, no collateral. Available everywhere.
- **Vehicle** - mid-size, lower interest. Available everywhere.
- **Business** - large, requires collateral. Maze and Pacific only.
- **Property** - biggest amounts, longest terms, requires collateral. Pacific only.

![Loan application](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Loans/1.png)

## Applying for a Loan

1. Open the Loans tab in the banking UI.
1. Pick the loan type, principal amount, and term length in months.
1. Your credit score is checked against the loan's minimum. Below threshold = denied.
1. Approved loans pay out instantly. Repayments (EMIs) are deducted on a fixed schedule.

## Credit Score

Scores range 300-900, starting at 650 for new accounts. Score improves with on-time loan repayments and active credit-card use; it drops with missed credit card payments and loan defaults.

## Missed Payments & Default

If auto-pay fails, the EMI is marked overdue. After 3 consecutive overdue EMIs the loan is flagged as **defaulted**, collateral (if any) is held, and a written-off scar is recorded after the grace period passes.

A reminder ping nags about defaulted loans on a slow cadence so it does not spam. Late EMIs paid back create a fading "scar" on your credit score that decays over time.
