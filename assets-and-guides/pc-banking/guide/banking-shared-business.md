# Shared & Business Accounts

## Shared Accounts

Open a shared account to pool funds with friends, family, or roommates. Each bank offers a shared product with different member caps:

- Fleeca Shared - up to 5 members.
- Maze Shared - up to 8 members.
- Pacific Shared - up to 10 members.

Members get an invite, must accept, and are added with a role. Roles control who can deposit, withdraw, manage members, and run payroll.

## Inviting and Managing Members

1. Open the shared account, go to **Members**, and invite by name or contact.
1. The invitee gets a request notification and must accept.
1. Once joined, assign a role. Default roles are Owner, Manager, Employee - each with its own permissions and withdraw cap.
1. Owners can remove members, change roles, or freeze the account; non-owners cannot.

## Business Accounts

Business accounts are for shops, contractors, and registered organisations. They support payroll, larger limits, and a higher cap on standing orders. Only one business account per player per bank.

Boss-level operations (withdraw, pay employees, edit balance) are gated behind role permissions. Employees see only what their role allows.
