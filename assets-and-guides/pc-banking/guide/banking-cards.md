# Bank Cards

## Debit and Credit

Cards live in your inventory as items. Each card is tied to an account and shows a holographic on-screen overlay when used. Two flavours:

- **Debit cards** draw straight from a linked checking account. iFruit Pay (entry, all banks), Fleeca Gold, and Maze Card.
- **Credit cards** charge against a credit line and are billed each cycle with interest on unpaid balance. Maze Gold, Lombank Platinum, Shark Elite, Pacific Black.

![Card overlay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Cards/1.png)

## Requesting a Card

1. Open the banking UI inside the right bank - only that bank's cards are available there.
1. Go to the Cards tab and pick a card. Issuance fees (if any) are deducted from your account.
1. The card item is added to your inventory with a unique number, last-four, and expiry.

Some cards charge a small annual fee. Premium cards (Pacific Black) require a higher-tier account.

![Cards screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Cards/2.png)

## PIN, Freeze, Limits

- **PIN** - set it on first issue, change it later from the card screen. PIN changes ask for 2FA.
- **Freeze / Unfreeze** - instantly block the card if it's lost or stolen. Frozen cards reject all transactions until you unfreeze.
- **Monthly & daily limits** - adjustable up to each card's max. Higher tiers allow bigger spending caps.
- **Lost card** - request a replacement; the old card is invalidated. A replacement fee may apply.

![Card detail](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Cards/3.png)

## Credit Card Lifecycle

Credit cards bill on a regular cycle. Pay at least the minimum (a percentage of balance) or the unpaid amount accrues interest. Auto-pay can deduct the minimum each cycle automatically.

Miss too many payments and the card freezes. Miss even more and the debt is written off and recorded as a credit-score scar.
