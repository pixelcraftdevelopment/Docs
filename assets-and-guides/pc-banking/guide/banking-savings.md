# Savings

## Opening a Savings Account

1. Walk into the bank that offers the savings tier you want, or use /bank nearby.
1. Go to the Savings tab and pick a product. Each tier has its own minimum opening deposit.
1. The account appears in your accounts list immediately and starts earning interest from the next accrual cycle.

![Open savings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Savings/1.png)

## Deposit and Withdraw

Move funds in either direction from the savings screen. The source can be cash on hand or any of your checking accounts. Daily limits and per-transaction caps are configurable per tier.

![Savings deposit/withdraw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Savings/2.png)

## Lock-in & Penalties

Premium tiers (Maze Business Reserve, Pacific Elite Reserve) hold your money for a lock-in period. Withdrawing early triggers a percentage penalty on the amount taken out. Standard tiers have no lock-in.

## Interest Accrual

Interest is credited on a recurring schedule (default ~7 real days, tunable). Rates range from 2% on basic Fleeca up to 7% on Pacific Elite. Interest is paid into the same savings balance and immediately starts earning more.
