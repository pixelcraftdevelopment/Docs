# Standing Orders

## Creating a Standing Order

A standing order is a recurring auto-payment from one of your accounts to a contact, business, or another of your own accounts.

1. Open the banking UI or phone app and go to **Standing Orders**.
1. Pick the source account, recipient, amount, and frequency (daily / weekly / monthly).
1. Confirm. Payments fire automatically on schedule until you pause or delete the order.

Each account has a cap on how many standing orders it can host (Fleeca: 3, Maze: 5, Pacific: 10, business accounts much higher).

## Missed Payments

If a payment fails (insufficient funds, frozen account), you get a notification. After 3 consecutive failures the order auto-pauses so it stops spamming retries - you can resume it once the issue is resolved.

Pre-payment reminders fire in three tiers spread across the cycle so you have time to top up before the next debit.

## Editing & Pausing

From the standing orders list you can edit the amount or frequency, pause the order without losing it, or delete it entirely. Changes push live to the phone app if you have it open.
