# Phone App

## App Registration

The phone app ships as a separate companion resource. Once it's running, the Banking app auto-registers against whichever phone is started - lb-phone, yseries, qs-smartphone (and pro), qb-phone, gksphone, 17mov_Phone, or npwd. Open your phone, find the Banking app, tap to launch.

![Phone app home](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/PhoneApp/1.png)

## Login & 2FA

First launch asks for your banking password. Sessions on the phone last 24 hours by default. 2FA is enforced per-profile so each character can independently turn it on. Multi-phone setups (player carries more than one) detect which phone is active and route notifications correctly.

![Phone login](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/PhoneApp/2.png)

## What's in the App

- **Home** - balances across all your accounts at a glance.
- **Cards** - view, freeze, change PIN, set limits.
- **Transfer** - send to a contact, account number, or to a player standing nearby.
- **Standing Orders** - create and manage recurring payments.
- **Bills & Requests** - pay incoming bills, accept or decline pending approvals.
- **Notifications** - full history of pushes (transfers, large transactions, security events).
- **Society Admin** - bosses can run payroll and manage members from the phone.

## Live Updates

When something changes - a new bill arrives, a request is accepted, a standing order updates - the app refreshes in the background without you reopening it. Light theme is also available for players who prefer a brighter look.
