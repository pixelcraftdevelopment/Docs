# Accounts Overview

## Personal Checking

Each player gets one checking account at each bank, opened automatically. This is the everyday account used for transfers, bills, card payments, and standing orders.

- **Fleeca Checking** - no fees, low daily limits.
- **Maze Bank Checking** - small monthly fee, modest interest, mid limits.
- **Pacific Standard Checking** - premium fees, higher interest, biggest limits.

![Accounts Overview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Accounts/1.png)

## Savings Accounts

Open a savings account at any bank to earn interest on your money. Each bank offers different tiers - basic options have no lock-in, while high-yield accounts may require a minimum opening deposit, hold your money for a lock-in period, or charge an early withdrawal penalty.

- Interest accrues on a regular cadence (default: roughly once per in-game month).
- Higher tiers (Pacific Elite, Maze Business Reserve) pay more but lock funds longer.
- Open multiple savings accounts to split goals - vacation fund, vehicle fund, etc.

## Shared and Business Accounts

Beyond personal accounts, you can open **Shared Accounts** with friends or family (multiple members, role-based access) and **Business Accounts** for shops or organisations. See the dedicated section for details.
