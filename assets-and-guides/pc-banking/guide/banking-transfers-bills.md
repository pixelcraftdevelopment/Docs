# Transfers & Bills

## Sending Money

Open the banking UI and use the transfer screen to send funds. Three ways:

- **Between your own accounts** - move money across your checking, savings, shared, and business accounts.
- **To a contact** - pick a saved contact, type the amount, send.
- **To an account number** - paste the recipient's number for a one-off transfer.

Large transfers (above the configured threshold, default $10,000) ask for a 2FA code. Server-wide daily caps and per-transfer limits are configurable.

![Transfer screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Transfers/1.png)

## Nearby Transfers

From the phone app, send money to a player standing next to you without needing their account number. The recipient gets a request notification and must accept within 5 minutes - no money moves until they confirm. Cancel anytime before they accept.

![Nearby transfer flow](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Transfers/2.png)

## Bills

Bills sent to you (rent, fines, vendor invoices) appear in the Bills tab. Pay from any of your accounts, or let standing orders handle them automatically. Bill history is kept for review.

- Push notification on new incoming bill.
- Overdue bills are flagged in the UI.
- Pay a bill in full or partially, depending on the issuer's settings.

![Due bills](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Transfers/3.png)

## Requests

Anything that needs your approval - nearby transfer offers, shared account invites, large pending bills - shows up in the Requests tab. Accept or decline; expired requests are cleared automatically.

![Requests inbox](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pixelcraftdevelopment/Guides/main/BankingGuide/images/Transfers/4.png)
